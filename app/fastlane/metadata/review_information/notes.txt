Daylie — Notes for App Review

(한국어 안내는 분량 제한으로 줄였습니다. 아래 영문과 같은 내용입니다.)

[English]
The app is a pair of phones.

1) The family (adult child) phone signs in with Google or Apple. It registers a
   parent, links the parent's phone with a QR code, then sees the parent's
   photos grouped by day (어제까지, "up to yesterday"), its own and the parent's
   photos of today as one diary (오늘하루, "today"), leaves a heart or a one-line
   comment, and sends one of its own photos to the parent from that photo's ⋯
   menu.
2) The parent's phone never creates an account. It is linked once, by scanning
   the QR code the family phone shows or by tapping a link the family sends.
   After that the app shows one large 사진 찍기 ("take a photo") button, which
   opens the phone's own camera. Having no menus, no settings and no sign-out
   on the parent's phone is intentional: an elderly parent presses one large
   button, takes the photo, and it reaches the family. "내 사진"
   (my photos) at the bottom-left shows their own photos; a family member's
   message pops up at the top and opens a reply screen when tapped.

The demo account provided in the "Sign-in required" fields of this review
information is a family (adult child) account. To use it, tap "이메일로
로그인" ("sign in with email") below the Google button on the sign-in screen.
It is already linked to a demo parent and already contains sample photos, so
signing in is enough to review everything: the photo list by day, a photo's
detail page (time, place, the automatically written memo), hearts and
comments, today's diary, and ⋯ → "부모님께 보내기" (send to parent). A second
device is not required.

To see the parent's side yourself: on the sign-in screen tap "부모님
휴대폰이에요" ("This is my parent's phone"), then tap "코드 입력" ("enter code")
and type the 8-character pairing code generated in the family account under
Settings → 부모님 (Parent). Right after linking, one consent screen appears
("가족에게 오늘 하루를 보여드릴까요?" — "Show your family your day?").

AI photo memos:
· The short title and description under each photo are written by Google's
  Gemini API. The parent's consent screen and the family's registration
  screen say so ("사진 설명은 Google의 AI가 써요. 사진이 Google에 전달되지만 AI
  학습에는 쓰이지 않아요" — the description is written by Google's AI; the
  photo is sent to Google but not used for training), and the privacy policy
  (§4, §6) describes it.

Permissions:
· Camera — to take the photo, and to read the QR code when linking.
· Location (while in use) — only to label the photo with the name of the place
  it was taken. Denying it is fine; the photo is still sent. No background or
  "always" location is requested.
· Photos — to also save the captured photo to the phone's own library.
· Notifications — the family phone gets new photos, replies and the evening
  summary; the parent's linked phone gets only photos and messages the family
  sent. The microphone is not used.

There are no health or medical features and the app makes no health claims. It
is a family photo-sharing app.
