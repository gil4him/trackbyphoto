How it works

Connect once. Then it's one tap.

2

One app, two screens

Simple for parents, rich for family

Parent's screen

Just a camera

  • No menus, no lists, no sign-in — open it and the camera is on
  • Text size: normal or large
  • Answer a message with one “Thank you” or a short reply
  • Photos from the family appear large on their screen
  • “My photos” shows everything they took in a large grid; photos also stay in the phone's Photos app
Family's screen

A sense of the day before you call

  • Every photo in that day's timeline, with the time and the name of the place
  • A short diary entry for each moment, written for you
  • The places they went that day, on a single map
  • “Today” shows your photos and your parent's together, in the order of the day
  • Send one of your own photos with a line of your own
  • A notification for each new photo and reply, and an evening count of the day's photos

FAQ

Questions?

Does my parent need an account?

No. Your parent never makes an ID or a password. Their phone connects by seeing the QR code on your phone, or by tapping the link you send.

We live far apart. Can we still connect?

Yes. Tap “Send the link” on your phone and send it by text message. Your parent taps it once; it takes them through installing the app and connecting.

Does location have to be allowed?

No. Location is used only to name the place a photo was taken. If it's not allowed, photos are still sent to the family.

Who writes the short diary under each photo?

Google's AI (Gemini) writes it from what's in the photo. Photos are sent only to write the note and are not used for AI training.

How much does it cost?

The basics are free. To keep photos longer and let more family members see them, you can subscribe to the Plus or Family plan in the app. They are monthly subscriptions and can be cancelled at any time in your store's subscription settings.
