# Daylie — rules for every Claude Code session

## Deploy & product decisions

### Memo tone: memory diary (CEO decision, final)
- Photo memos use the **memory diary** tone, not photo description. Memos are written by Gemini (`cloudMemo` ON); the local model is the fallback. The prompt is `worker/src/llm/prompt.ts` (default branch of `buildPrompt`).
- **memo** (title, 12–24 chars) = what the parent did + where. If a landmark, tourist spot or intentionally framed background is visible, its name comes first and the title ends with "!" ("불국사에 다녀오셨어요!"). No "!" otherwise.
- **scene** = one sentence (max 2, ≤50 chars) weaving 1–2 memory anchors with the action: real weather/temperature (WeatherKit, never guessed from the photo), weekday/holiday, part of day, a trip far from home, the flow from earlier photos today.
  - "맑고 따뜻한 일요일, 점심을 드시고 나와 일행과 기념사진을 남기셨어요."
  - "비가 내리는 쌀쌀한 월요일 아침, 우산을 쓰고 출근길에 나서셨어요."
- Never list or describe objects/background ("식탁 위에 물잔이 놓여 있어요", "뒤로 상점들이 보여요"). Never invent feelings ("맛있는", "즐거운", "예쁜"), nor feeling adverbs on the action ("편안하게", "여유롭게", "즐겁게", "맛있게"; stripped in code by `dropFeelingWords`). Code drops background/inventory sentences (except the first): `tidyScene` / `isBackgroundSentence`.
- The `memoDiary` mode (description-first diary) stays in the code for `scripts/memo-diary-compare.ts` only. The pipeline never uses it, whatever the flag says.
- **Changing the memo tone or any prompt requires the CEO's explicit approval in the current conversation.**

### Parents never log in (owner decision, 2026-10-22)
- A parent's phone is linked only by the family's QR/link (managed record, no Google/Apple account). Google/Apple/email sign-in is for family members. Adding a parent's own Google login was considered and declined.
- A link stays until a family member taps 연결 해제, removes the parent, or deletes the account. Signing out never unlinks.
- Simple edition: the sign-in note says "로그인은 자녀용이에요. 부모님은 위의 ‘부모님 휴대폰이에요’를 눌러 주세요."; a signed-in account with no parent shows "부모님이신가요? 이 계정은 자녀용이에요. 자녀에게 받은 연결 링크로 들어오세요." with 부모님 휴대폰이에요 (signs out, opens the parent's way in).
- 자녀 vs 가족 (owner decision, 2026-10-28): the one person who sets up and connects the parent's phone is **자녀** — the parent's start screen (자녀예요, 자녀가 보낸 링크, 자녀 휴대폰의 QR), the pairing/approval screens and their errors, the camera-permission line, the sign-in notes above, and the parent consent line "자녀 휴대폰에서 언제든 연결을 끊을 수 있어요." The group stays **가족**: 함께 보는 가족, 가족 초대, 가족 n명, who sent a photo (가족이 보낸 사진), the consent/제3자 제공 text, invites (a 며느리 or grandchild may join), 대표 가족, and the sign-in tagline. English is unchanged ("family"). The landing site and store listings may say 자녀 for the story.

### 부모님 앱 업데이트 알림 (owner decision, 2026-10-27, 1.0.4)
- A parent's phone reports its app version and platform on open and on every return to the app (`reportDevice`, the only other request a parent's phone may make besides `registerFcmToken`; stored at `users/{p}/devices/{id}.app`). The latest store version per platform is `system/app`, written hourly by the worker from the App Store lookup and the Play production track (`worker/src/storeVersions.ts`); `admin_config/app.{ios,android}` typed by the owner in the console overrides it. An unknown version never shows "옛 버전".
- Family's Settings (each parent's row, and the parent's 휴대폰 section): "{이름} 휴대폰 앱이 옛 버전이에요" with **업데이트 알림 보내기** (guardian/admin only, `sendUpdateNudge`, once per parent per 24 h, auditLogs `device.updateNudge`) and **링크 보내기** (the store link through KakaoTalk or the share sheet; viewers get only this). Phones that report no version (1.0.3 and older) show "앱 버전 확인 안 됨" with both buttons. Up to date: "앱 1.0.4 · 최신 버전".
- Push to the parent's phone (type `app.update`, owner-approved wording): a phone that reported a version gets "새 버전이 나왔어요. 눌러서 업데이트해 주세요" (English parents: "A new version is out. Tap to update.") and a tap opens its store page; a phone that reported none gets "새 버전이 나왔어요. 앱스토어에서 업데이트해 주세요" / "…Play 스토어에서 업데이트해 주세요" (its tap only opens the app). Changing these wordings needs the owner's approval.
- The parent's phone shows "새 버전이 나왔어요 · 눌러서 업데이트" at the top of every screen while it is behind; no close button, it goes away once updated.
- firestore.rules: `reportDevice`, `sendUpdateNudge` added to the `requests` allow-list (owner-approved, 2026-10-27). privacy §2 lists 앱 버전 and the update notice (시행일 2026-10-28).
- Release (owner, 2026-10-27): 1.0.3 is skipped in both stores; 1.0.4 (build 29885479) goes out instead, and its "What's New" carries 1.0.3's two calendar-fix lines before the update-notice line.

### 미접속·미수락 알림 (owner decision, 2026-10-29, simple edition)
- Two automatic reminders in `worker/src/handlers/nudges.ts`, each behind its own 운영실 flag, shipped OFF: `absenceNudges` and `pendingLinkNudges`. Both fire only between 09:00 and 21:00 in the parent's zone (digest tz).
- **24시간 미접속** (`absenceNudges`): a parent's linked phone has not opened Daylie for 24 h (no `reportDevice` and no photo). The parent's phone gets one push (type `absence.parent`, a tap opens the app): "가족이 오늘 사진을 기다리고 있어요. 눌러서 사진 한 장 남겨 주세요" / EN "Your family is waiting for today's photo. Tap to take one." Every family member gets a notice + push (type `absence.family`): "{이름} 휴대폰에서 하루 넘게 Daylie를 열지 않았어요. 전화 한 통 드려 보시는 건 어때요?" / EN "{name} hasn't opened Daylie in over a day. How about a quick call?" At most once per 24 h while the absence lasts (`users/{p}/private/absenceNudge.lastSentAt`); it ends by itself when the phone opens. Phones that never reported a version are never nudged. From 1.0.5 the parent's phone reports an open once an hour (`appVersion.shouldReport`); phones on 1.0.4 report once a day, so for them a report counts as stale only after 48 h.
- **12시간 미수락** (`pendingLinkNudges`): a parent connect link (`pairings`, onboard, still pending) or a family invite (`invites`, unused) made 12–24 h ago reminds the person who made it exactly once (notice + push; ids `pairing_pending_{id}`, `invite_pending_{sha}`). Connect link: "{이름} 휴대폰이 아직 연결되지 않았어요. 전화로 도와드리거나 설정에서 링크를 다시 보내 주세요" / EN "{name}'s phone isn't connected yet. Help them over the phone, or send the link again from Settings." Family invite: "보내신 가족 초대를 아직 아무도 받지 않았어요. 초대 코드는 24시간만 쓸 수 있으니 한 번 더 알려 주세요" / EN "Nobody has accepted your family invite yet. The code works for 24 hours, so you may want to remind them." Tapping either opens that parent's 설정. Links the family replaced with a new one and 1 h re-link codes get no reminder.
- {이름} is how the family addresses the parent (엄마, 영희님; English drops 님). Changing any of these wordings, windows or recipients needs the owner's approval. privacy §2 (알림 정보) lists the parent-phone absence reminder (시행일 2026-11-05, ko + en).

### Deploying
- Never deploy uncommitted code. Order: commit → push to main (or merge the PR) → deploy from an up-to-date checkout of main, preferably a worktree from `origin/main`.
- `worker/scripts/install.sh` and `simple/worker/install-simple.sh` enforce this (`--check` runs the guard alone; `FORCE_DEPLOY=1` only in emergencies, and tell the user).
- Before deploying, read the running commit (`~/daylie-worker-simple/DEPLOYED`, `~/trackbyphoto-worker/DEPLOYED`) and make sure your checkout contains it: `git merge-base --is-ancestor <deployed> HEAD`. If not, stop and ask: someone else's work would be lost.
- After a deploy, verify from the worker log (`~/Library/Logs/daylie-worker-simple.log`, `~/Library/Logs/trackbyphoto-worker.log`) that the `[worker] starting` line shows the expected `deployedCommit` and features (e.g. `"weather":"WeatherKit"`), and tell the user what is running.
- Production deploys (workers, Firebase, store submissions) are run by the user unless they say otherwise.
- Landing site (www.daylieai.com): deploys from `landing/` with `cd landing && firebase deploy --only hosting --project daylie-simple-4df13` to Hosting site `daylieai`, separate from the app sites (`dayliesimple` etc.); it has no build step and never touches app/, simple/, workers or rules.
