Article 2 (What the Service Is)

Daylie is a service through which a family knows about a parent's day from the photos the parent takes.

  • The parent's phone — A simple photo app where the camera turns on as soon as you open it. Parents do not log in; they are connected just once, with a QR code or a one-time connection code that the family shows them.
  • The family's (adult child's) phone — See the parent's photos along with the time, the place name, and the photo memo; see the places they went; leave a short message; and send their own photos to the parent.
  • Notifications — We notify the family when a photo arrives, and once in the evening with the news of the day's photos. The parent's phone is notified only of photos and messages the family sent.

The basic features are free. Paid plans that keep photos longer and let more family members view them are governed by Article 9.
