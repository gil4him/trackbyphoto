Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Daylie is an app that lets a family know how their parent's day went, through the photos the parent takes. This policy explains, in plain words, what information Daylie receives, what it is used for, and when it is deleted.

In short, the only people who see the photos and where they were taken are the connected family. We do not hand the information to anyone other than the service providers we need in order to run the service (Section 5), we do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it to other companies.

1. How the service is put together

Daylie works as a pair of two sides.

  • The family member's (adult child's) phone — they sign in with a Google or Apple account, create a family, link the parent's phone, and view the parent's photos.
  • The parent's phone — a simple photo app that opens straight into the camera. The parent does not sign in. The phone is linked once, using a QR code or a one-time link code shown by the family member, and after that it runs only as an anonymous session, with no name, email address, or phone number.

When the parent's phone is being linked, the app asks the parent once, "Shall we show your family how your day went?" Photos are sent to the family only after the parent taps to agree.

Daylie does not collect resident registration numbers (Korean national ID numbers), does not collect payment information, and does not use advertising identifiers. There is no field for entering health conditions or diagnoses. We have not included any usage analytics tool or crash-reporting tool.

About location information. Daylie records only the location at the moment a photo is taken, attached to that photo. It does not follow and log the phone's location continuously. Photos are delivered normally even if location permission is not granted; in that case only the time is kept, with no place name. The Places visited (다녀온 곳) screen is drawn on the family member's phone on the spot, by connecting the locations attached to that day's photos; no separate route history is stored.

3. Why we use personal information

  • To show the connected family the photos the parent took and where they were taken
  • To arrange photos in time order and show place names and places visited alongside them
  • To notify the family when a photo is uploaded, and to send one evening update about the day's photos ("3 photos from Mom today")
  • To show the short messages (한마디) left by the family on the parent's phone, and to pass the parent's replies to the family
  • To link a family member's phone and the parent's phone to each other, to unlink them, and to delete accounts and photos
  • To find and fix problems where the service stops working or photos do not upload

We do not use the information for anything beyond the purposes above. If a purpose changes, we will tell you in advance and ask for your consent again.

4. About the AI that writes photo notes

The title of a photo (a single line such as "Lunch with soybean paste stew") and its description are written automatically by AI. The description either records what can be seen, in a few sentences, or is written as a short diary entry about that moment. A diary entry may also include the date and time of day the photo was taken and a rough area name (for example, "October 18, afternoon, in Gangnam"), and only the connected family can see it. Reading information out of a photo and writing the description happen in the two places below.

  • On the phone itself (iPhone). When a photo is taken, the phone's own built-in features read out object names, text in the photo, and the number of faces. The photo does not leave the phone for this work, and only the results that were read out are stored.
  • On a computer the operator manages directly (the default). Photo notes are written by an AI model running on the operator's own server (a Mac mini). On this path, photos are not sent to any outside AI provider.

Daylie currently does not send photos to any outside cloud AI provider. If we come to use one, we will revise this policy and tell you before we do. The operator does not train AI models on users' photos or writing.

Sentences written by AI can be wrong. A photo note is only an aid to help you recognize a photo quickly; it is not a judgment about health or illness.

5. Processors we entrust with handling data, and disclosure to third parties

Daylie does not sell personal information. In order to run the service, we entrust processing work to the providers below.

Provider Work entrusted
Google LLC (Firebase) Sign-in (Authentication), data storage (Firestore), photo storage (Cloud Storage), notification delivery (Cloud Messaging), website serving (Hosting)
Apple Inc. Sign in with Apple (only if you sign in that way)
OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim) Converting the coordinates where a photo was taken into an address and the name of a shop or place, and finding a home address from the search text typed in Settings.
komoot GmbH (Photon) Suggesting addresses while a home address is being typed in Settings. We send the typed search text and approximate coordinates (the home location or the latest photo's location, to about 100 m) so nearby places come first.

For place-name and address lookups we send only coordinates or the address search text. We do not send the photo, a name, an email address, or an account identifier. The lookup is made from the operator's server, so your phone does not connect to these providers directly.

If you tap a map link on a photo, Google Maps or Apple Maps opens, depending on your phone. In that case only that point's coordinates (and the place name) are passed along in the URL; no photo or account information is passed. If you send a link through KakaoTalk, the message is delivered from your own phone through the KakaoTalk app.

Apart from cases where investigative authorities or others require it through lawful process under the law, we do not provide personal information to any third party other than those above.

7. How long we keep personal information, and how we destroy it

Photos

Photos and the records attached to them are kept until you delete them or delete the account or the parent's records. You can delete a photo yourself in the app at any time.

Photos are not deleted automatically after a set period. If we begin automatic deletion, we will tell you in this policy and inside the app before it starts.

Accounts and family records

  • If you delete your account, your account information, your family records, and that family's photos and the records attached to them are all deleted. You can do this yourself at Settings → Delete account (설정 → 계정 삭제) in the app. You may also request it by email at the address in Section 13, and we will verify, delete, and tell you the result.
  • Deleting a parent's records can be done by the family member (guardian) who registered the parent, at Settings → Delete parent (설정 → 부모님 삭제) in the app. Deleting removes that parent's sign-in credentials, photos, photo records, short messages and replies, consent records, family link, and notification records, and cannot be undone. If you only break the link (unlink), the parent's phone immediately becomes unable to send photos, and photos already received remain.
  • For a one-time link code we store only the hash; a code for the first link expires after 24 hours and a code for re-linking expires after 1 hour, after which it cannot be used.
  • If you turn notifications off or delete the app from the device, the push token is no longer used, and tokens that fail delivery are cleaned up automatically.

How we destroy data: electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered. It can take a short time for data to be removed from backup copies. If there is a record the law requires us to keep, we keep it separately only for the period that law sets and then delete it. Daylie does not take payments, so at present there are no transaction records kept separately under this policy.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You (and the family member acting on a parent's behalf) may exercise the rights below at any time.

  • Access your own personal information — you can view photos and records directly in the app.
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete — photos from the photo screen, the whole account from settings, and a parent's records from Settings → Delete parent
  • Stop the processing — turn notifications off, turn location permission off, break the link
  • Withdraw consent — a parent can do so by telling their family or by deleting the app; a family member can do so by breaking the link or deleting the account

If it is hard to do this yourself in the app, let us know at the contact below and we will verify, carry it out, and tell you the result. We will act within 10 days of the day we receive the request.

9. Children under 14

Daylie is not directed at children under 14, and we do not accept sign-ups from children under 14. If we learn that personal information of a child under 14 has come in, we delete it right away.

10. Measures to keep data secure

  • All communication is encrypted (HTTPS), and stored data and photos are kept encrypted as well.
  • Photos and records are locked down by security rules, so only connected family accounts can read them.
  • Server access is given only to the minimum number of people needed to run the service.
  • The computer that creates photo notes is managed directly by the operator; the work happens only in memory and no copy of the photo is left on disk.

11. Advertising and selling data

Daylie has no ads. We have not included any ad network or behavioral tracking tool, and we do not sell or rent personal information to other companies.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post what changed and the effective date on this page. If a change is unfavorable to users, we will also give separate notice inside the app before the effective date.
